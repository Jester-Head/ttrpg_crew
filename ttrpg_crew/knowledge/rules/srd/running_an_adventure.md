# RUNNING AN ADVENTURE

As the Game Master (GM), you are the players’ guide through the world of Daggerheart. You present interesting challenges, embody the inhabitants of the world, and help the players tell a collaborative story.

## GM PRINCIPLES & BEST PRACTICES

To get the most out of Daggerheart, we recommend GMs keep the following principles and practices in mind throughout each session:

### PRINCIPLES

*   Be a fan of the player characters.
*   Address the characters and address the players.
*   Build the world together.
*   Play to find out what happens.
*   Hold on gently.

### BEST PRACTICES

*   Let the story breathe.
*   Embrace the chaos.
*   Telegraph danger.
*   Reward creativity.

*For more information, see the Daggerheart Core Rulebook, pages 108 and 147.*

## GM MOVES

When the spotlight swings to you, make a **GM move** that follows your principles and best practices. A GM move can be anything that logically follows from the fiction of the scene and moves the story forward. You should make a GM move when:

*   A player rolls with Fear on an action roll.
*   A player fails an action roll.
*   A player does something that would have consequences.
*   A player gives you a golden opportunity.
*   A player looks to you for what happens next.

When you make a GM move, you can also spend any Fear you’ve accumulated to activate a **Fear Feature** from an adversary or environment, or to seize the spotlight and make an additional move.

After you’ve made your move, the spotlight swings back to the players.
