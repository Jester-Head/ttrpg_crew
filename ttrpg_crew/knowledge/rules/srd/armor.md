# ARMOR

All armor has a tier and an Armor Score, and some armors have features.

## ARMOR SCORE & ARMOR SLOTS

An armor’s **Armor Score** represents its ability to reduce incoming physical damage. Each time a character takes physical damage, they can mark any number of their armor’s **Armor Slots** to reduce the incoming damage by an amount equal to their armor’s Armor Score for each slot marked.

Each armor has a number of Armor Slots, which are listed on the armor’s card. Once all of an armor’s slots are marked, it is broken and can no longer be used to reduce incoming damage. Broken armor must be fully repaired before it can be used again.

> _Note:_ _An armor’s Armor Score only applies to incoming physical damage; it can’t be used to reduce magic damage._

## DAMAGE THRESHOLDS

Your damage thresholds are calculated by adding your current level to the values listed on your armor card.

## FEATURE

An armor’s **feature** is a special rule that stays in effect while the armor is equipped.
