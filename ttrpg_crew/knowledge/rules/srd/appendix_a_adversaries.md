# APPENDIX A: ADVERSARIES

This appendix contains a selection of adversaries for the GM to use in their adventures. Each entry includes a stat block with the adversary’s tier, type, HP, and other important information.

## ADVERSARY STAT BLOCKS

Each adversary stat block includes the following information:

*   **Tier:** The adversary’s tier, which determines its overall power level.
*   **Type:** The adversary’s type, such as Beast, Humanoid, or Undead.
*   **HP:** The adversary’s Hit Points.
*   **Difficulty:** The Difficulty of attack rolls made against the adversary.
*   **Damage Thresholds:** The adversary’s damage thresholds.
*   **Actions:** The actions the adversary can take.
*   **Fear Features:** The special abilities the adversary can use by spending Fear.
*   **Description:** A brief description of the adversary.

## TIER 1 ADVERSARIES

### GIANT RAT

*Tier 1 Beast*

**HP:** 3  
**Difficulty:** 10  
**Damage Thresholds:** Major 5 / Severe 8

**Actions:**

*   **Bite:** Melee, d6 phy

**Fear Features:**

*   **Swarm (1 Fear):** If there are three or more Giant Rats, they can make a group attack. All targets in Melee range must succeed on a reaction roll (12) or take d6 phy damage.
