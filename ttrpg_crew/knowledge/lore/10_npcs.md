### Clara Moree, Canonical Backstory

Clara Moree's recovery from Calcination was spontaneous and unprecedented. She was calcined, became a Dreg, and then slowly, over months or years, started coming back. Nobody knows why. She is the only documented case. Her entire research career is an attempt to understand and replicate what happened to her naturally.

#### Implications

- Her Living Memory research isn't what saved her, it's what she developed *because* she was saved and wants to know how.
- The calx-based experiments are a step toward reverse-engineering her own recovery.
- The mystery of *why* Clara recovered remains open, and is one of the most important unanswered questions in Gloamvere's medical/alchemical history.
- Clara is simultaneously a source of hope ("recovery is possible") and resentment ("she got out, why can't the rest of us?").

#### Existing Lore Preserved

Her position as Fellow in the Clade's Scholar track, her willingness to authorize calx for construct programming, her contradictory relationship with Command, and her political precariousness all remain as documented in Part VII, Factions & Power Structures and Part IV, Memory Economy & Technology.

### Established NPCs by Faction

*(Full personality profiles, district assignments, and antagonist roles are open development items.)*

#### Spiritus Conclave
- **Resonant Teru Ironwright**: Pragmatist leader (Shaper-aligned). Believes every memory deserves a home. Slowly becoming consonant with their workshop, fingers are fine silver needles for weaving memory into silk.
- **Resonant Sabine Threadwright**: Purist (Tender-aligned). Garments are the highest status symbols in Gloamvere. Obsessed with informed consent.
- **Athanor Juzo the Silent**: Smithwright who achieved Consonance with his hammer. No longer speaks, claiming he must remain silent to "hear" the spiritus.
- **Bound Miri of the Dregs**: Young apprentice who finds beauty in Leached memories, infusing them into chime-stones that play fragments of forgotten lullabies.

#### Old Blood Council
- **Patriarch Theodore Vaughan**: Living Reliquary and mouthpiece for five generations of ancestors. Often argues with his own internal voices during negotiations.
- **High Priestess Florina**: Rigid traditionalist of the Ashen Ancestors. Considers memory modification "spiritual butchery."
- **Sister Noelle**: Shroud-Bearer of Lahkrima with violet eyes from absorbing too much trauma. A saint to the Dregs, slowly losing her own sense of self.
- **Maddox the Apostate**: Secret scion and Hollow Flame member. Seeks to crack open the noble vaults to redistribute historical warmth by force.

#### Siderian Clade
- **Fellow Clara Moree**: Former Dreg who recovered spontaneously (see above). Leads Calcination-reversal research. Authorizes calx for construct programming. Simultaneously indispensable to Command and distrusted by her own track.
- **Architect Harlon**: Runs the Memory Refineries. Views memory purely as data and fuel. Famously dismisses nostalgia as "inefficient fuel."
- **Fellow Jian**: Researcher obsessed with the Veil. Experiments with High-Arousal Tinctura to pierce the boundaries of Kavath.
- **Luminary "The Warden"**: Command division head overseeing construct deployment. Believes a strong Clade is the only thing preventing civil war.

#### Hollow Flame
- **Jessa Flowers**: Former Shroud-Beareress. The movement's most articulate moderate voice.
- **Cask**: Scarred, nameless Fracturer who claims to have cracked eleven preservation seals.
- **Wren**: Active Reminiscent who secretly feeds vault schedules to the moderate wing.
- **Old Kessel**: Former Remnant dismissed after forty years of vault service.

#### Historical / Other
- **Lady Nyxentia**: Mistress of Discord worshiped by the Cult of the Fractured Mirror.
