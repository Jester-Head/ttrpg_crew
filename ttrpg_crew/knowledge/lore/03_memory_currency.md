# Part III, Memory Currency System

## Memory Currency System
*Canonical Reference, v3.1*

**System Status:** Active 
**Aesthetic Standard:** Alchemical/Cognitive 
**Naming Convention:** v3.0 (Volatilis through Athanor)

---

### 1. The Seven-Tier Hierarchy & Conversion Standards

The currency operates on a tiered system of complexity. Higher tiers are exponentially rarer, harder to stabilize, and less fungible.

**Important distinction:**
- **Tiers 1–4** are *market-fungible* (priced by grade, purity, and stability).
- **Tiers 5–7** are *content-bound* (treated as unique assets, traded by contract/barter, not “priced” cleanly).

| Tier | Name | Nature | Definition | Trade Value (Approximate) |
|:---:|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Volatilis** | Sensory | A fleeting sensation (scent, sound, color). Evaporates quickly. | **Base unit.** Small change. |
| **2** | **Solvate** | Working | A passing thought or “solution” of an idea. Unstable. | **~10 Volatilis.** A simple meal. |
| **3** | **Amalga** | Procedural | “Muscle memory.” Fused skills (lockpicking, swordplay). | **~5 Solvate.** A good tool or weapon. |
| **4** | **Precipia** | Semantic | Objective facts/expertise. Highly stable. | **~4 Amalga.** A small house or rare relic. |
| **5** | **Tinctura** | Episodic | A deep extraction of a specific lived event with emotional context. | **Barter/contract.** Value = content + intensity + provenance. |
| **6** | **Quintessa** | Identity | The “core Ego” (deep identity continuity). | **Priceless.** Life debts, artifacts, sealed truths, succession claims. |
| **7** | **Athanor** | Universal | “World memories” beyond any verified record (pre-history / pre-human / pre-Arche by rumor). | **Mythic.** Districts, armies, eras, if real. |

> **Outside the hierarchy (not tiers):**
> - **Caput Mortuum:** fuel-grade waste at the true endpoint after repeated burn/consumption/refinement cycles. 
> - **Essentia:** an addictive emotional byproduct strongly associated with **Clade** industrial refinement. 
> Neither is a currency tier. Both are detailed in the Memory Economy & Technology Pipeline (`04a`) and the Master Glossary.

### 2. Degradation Rules & Manifestations

Degradation is the entropy pressure that shapes Gloamvere City’s economy. Memory is a substance, but it is not inert: it is sensitive to handling, exchange, contamination, and repeated consumption/re-extraction.

#### Canonical Rule (Market Standard)

A memory degrades when it undergoes **unanchored handling**, especially:
- **frequent trade/circulation**
- **transfer between containers** (particularly poor or unsealed vessels)
- **consumption and re-extraction cycles**
- **exposure to mixed Drift environments** (cross-contamination)

**Preservation (“Anchoring”) halts degradation at its current state but cannot reverse it.**

#### The Degradation Ladder (Common Descriptors)

1. **Pristine:** First-hand, never circulated. Perfect clarity. The “owner” feels the wind on their skin.
2. **Clear:** 1–2 circulations. Minor blur; meaning remains intact.
3. **Faded:** Multiple circulations. **Faces genericize.** Dialogue paraphrases. Sensory detail dulls.
4. **Distorted:** Heavy circulation. **Emotional bleed** and contamination. Key facts drift or become wrong.
5. **Leached:** Near-collapse. “Grey” memory; context mostly gone. Often sold as **low-grade lamp stock**.
6. **Spent / Ashing (informal):** Not a tier, an observed condition where repeated burn/processing leaves only inert residue.
7. **Caput Mortuum:** Endpoint waste. Useful as fuel and industrial filler; worthless as experience.

> **Terminology note (Drift):** 
> **Drift** = the symptomset (emotional bleed, sensory flashes, intrusive affect). 
> **Canal Drift** = Drift driven by refinery runoff in city waterways. 
> **Wild Drift** = Drift driven by ambient memory saturation outside the city.
> **Environmental Drift** = accelerated degradation of carried memories near Thin Spots and on Unmoored Paths.

### 3. Consumption Mechanics & Side Effects

Consumption is the act of uncorking a vial and inhaling the vapor/smoke.

#### Mechanics (In-World)

- **Speed:** Absorption is effectively instantaneous.
- **Experience:** The user does not “watch” the memory; they **live** it. For a heartbeat, they *are* the origin mind.

#### Side Effects & Risks

- **Psychological addiction:** High-arousal **Tinctura** is habit-forming. Users chase clean emotion in a world of grey.
- **Memory bleed (Drift):** Excessive consumption blurs identity boundaries; users confuse absorbed memories with their own.
- **Cerebral overload:** Consuming a memory significantly above one’s tolerance can stun, fracture attention, or induce panic/shock. 
 *(GM: call for an appropriate Daggerheart roll and apply Stress/complications on failure.)*

#### The Horror of Calcination (Quintessa)

- **The victim:** When a **Quintessa** is removed, the victim becomes a **Dreg**, a living body with no ego continuity, capable only of basic function.
- **The consumer:** Absorbing a Quintessa risks **Overwrite**. The host must win a contest of wills, or the consumed identity takes control.

> Calcination is the single most morally radioactive practice in Gloamvere City’s economy. 
> It is never “just trade.” It is identity violence.

#### The Reliquary Rite (Managed Quintessa Consumption)

The Old Blood Council’s Living Reliquary sacrament is the one sanctioned exception to the Overwrite rule. A Reliquarian who consumes an ancestral Quintessa under ritual conditions achieves **coexistence** rather than a contest of wills. The mechanism depends on three factors working together:

**1. Bloodline resonance.** The Old Blood’s border architecture (the Law of Blood) demonstrates that ancestral Tinctura recognizes descendent bloodlines and responds to them as “known.” The same principle operates during the Reliquary Rite: a preserved Quintessa consumed by a direct descendent does not treat the host as a rival body, it identifies the host as *kin*. The ancestor’s identity settles alongside the host’s rather than displacing it. This is why the rite is restricted to verified bloodline matches. Consuming a non-ancestral Quintessa carries full Overwrite risk regardless of preparation.

**2. Anchored state.** Vault-preserved Quintessa has been stabilized through Anchoring, its emotional volatility is dampened, its structure locked. A freshly extracted Quintessa is raw and reactive; a preserved one is settled. The Anchored state reduces (but does not eliminate) the intensity of the identity’s initial contact with the host, giving bloodline resonance time to establish coexistence rather than conflict.

**3. Ritual preparation at a Thin Spot.** The rite is performed at a Thin Spot under controlled conditions, the same liminal environment used for Preservation. The Reliquarian is guided through a staged absorption overseen by a senior Living Reliquary (or, in rare cases, an Exalted Scion who has undergone the process multiple times). The overseer monitors the integration and can intervene if the ancestor’s identity resists settling.

**What coexistence feels like:** Living Reliquaries describe a spectrum. At best, the ancestor is a persistent internal voice, advisory, opinionated, occasionally intrusive. At worst, the ancestor is a second will pressing against every decision. Patriarch Theodore Vaughan carries five generations and openly argues with them during negotiations. The coexistence is permanent and irreversible: the ancestor’s identity cannot be re-extracted without calcining the host.

**Failure modes (rare but documented):**
- **Partial Overwrite:** The ancestor’s personality dominates in specific emotional states (rage, grief, crisis). The host “loses themselves” intermittently. More common when the bloodline match is distant or the Quintessa was imperfectly Anchored.
- **Rejection:** The ancestor’s identity refuses to settle, persistent dissonance, cognitive noise, inability to focus. The host functions but never achieves true coexistence. Treated as a theological failure and a personal shame.
- **Full Overwrite:** Extremely rare under ritual conditions but not impossible. If the ancestor’s will was exceptionally strong and the descendent’s sense of self was already compromised (pre-existing Drift, emotional crisis, coercion), the ancestor may take control. The Old Blood does not discuss these cases publicly. The Hollow Flame claims to know of at least two.

> The Reliquary Rite is the reason the Old Blood values bloodline purity with such intensity. It is not mere snobbery, it is a practical requirement for the faction’s most sacred and most powerful practice. A strong, verified bloodline is the difference between gaining an ancestor’s wisdom and losing yourself to it.

### 4. Preservation Ritual (“Anchoring”)

Preservation halts degradation but cannot restore what has already been lost. It locks a memory in its current state (e.g., a “Preserved Faded Tinctura”).

#### Canonical Requirements

1. **Location:** Must be performed at a **Thin Spot**, a structured, liminal fault where the Veil is thinnest (doorframes with no door, archways, open graves, empty rooms). 
 *(See: Cosmology & Metaphysics v1.1, Thin Spots are “doors,” not raw Veil contact.)*
2. **Tool:** An **Eidolon Compass** to locate the memory’s emotional core and stabilize orientation.
3. **Catalyst:** A sacrificed memory that shares a property with the target (e.g., burning a Volatilis of “cold” to anchor a Tinctura of “winter”).

#### Visual Marker

A preserved memory shows a distinct **sparkle/glitter** within the smoke, visible without instruments. In markets, this becomes both authentication method and status signal.

### 5. Emotional Intensity Modifiers (Tinctura)

The value of a **Tinctura** is heavily modified by emotional content.

| Intensity | Examples | Stability | Market Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low Arousal** | Boredom, Serenity, Acceptance | High (degrades slowly) | **Low.** Hard to sell. |
| **Mid Arousal** | Joy, Sadness, Trust | Moderate | **Standard.** |
| **High Arousal** | Rage, Terror, Ecstasy, Grief | Low (volatile) | **High.** Sought by addicts, warriors, extremists. |
| **Conflicted** | Bittersweet, Guilt, Relief-with-shame | Very Low | **Discounted.** “Impure,” contamination-prone. |

### 6. Faction-Specific Views & Practice

Currency names are standardized city-wide, but **the ethics and methods** of memory trade vary by faction and district.

#### Spiritus Conclave

- **View:** **Artisan/animist.** Memory is *spiritus*, living essence that can be housed, shaped, and respected.
- **Practice:** Masters of **infusion craft**, curated lanternwork, and high-quality containment. Conclave phialwright standards underpin every “legitimate” market because stable containers are a civic necessity.
- **Taboo:** **Careless waste** and disrespect of spiritus. Reckless handling that drives memory to leached/spent states is considered a craft failure (and, privately, a moral one). Calcination is treated as monstrous even when “legal.”

#### Old Blood Council

- **View:** **Sacred/ancestral.** Memory is lineage, burial, and covenant with the dead.
- **Practice:** Hoards **Tinctura** and **Quintessa** for preservation, succession, and reliquary rites. Trades are framed as obligations and inheritance, not commerce.
- **Taboo:** **Modification.** Refining, blending, and “improving” memory is treated as desecration. Burning memory as fuel, especially high-tier, is sacrilege in doctrine (even if hypocrisy exists in practice).

#### Siderian Clade

- **View:** **Mechanical/empirical.** Memory is measurable data with exploitable properties.
- **Practice:** Leads industrial **classification, stabilization research, and large-scale refinement**. Uses **Precipia** and **Amalga** for training protocols, construct programming, and operational advantage. Essentia is strongly associated with Clade pipelines.
- **Taboo:** **Emotion without utility.** Unstructured Tinctura is treated as hazardous noise unless weaponized, studied, or reduced to controllable outputs.
