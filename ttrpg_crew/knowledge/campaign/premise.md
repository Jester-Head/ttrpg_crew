# Campaign Premise

*Edit freely, this is the anchor the GM frames every scene around.*

Gloamvere City, torn from the sunlit world into the twilight demi-plane of
Kavath, runs on memory, extracted, graded, and spent as currency. Three powers
divide the trade: the **Old Blood Council** (who hoard ancestral memory in
vaulted Ossuaries), the **Spiritus Conclave** (artisans who weave memory into
crafted things), and the **Siderian Clade** (engineers who refine it into fuel
and constructs). Beneath them drift the **Hollow Flame**, who believe sacred
memory belongs to everyone, and the **Dregs**, hollowed when their core selves
were stripped away.

You are **Shimoda Rice**, a Canalborne **courier**: a neutral carrier of corked
memory-vials between districts that trust no one else's messengers. You go
everywhere and belong nowhere, which is exactly why everyone uses you. But the
work has marked you. You have begun to **Fray**, the first stage of the Dreg
Condition. The only hope on record is **Fellow Clara Moree**, the single soul
ever to come back. The city will keep asking you to carry other people's pasts;
the question is how much of your own you have left to lose.
