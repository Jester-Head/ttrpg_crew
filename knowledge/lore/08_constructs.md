# Part VIII, Construct Technology

## Construct Technology
*Canonical Reference, v1.1*

**Status:** Active 
**Aesthetic Standard:** Alchemical/Cognitive 
**Naming Convention:** v3.0 (Volatilis through Athanor)

---

### What Constructs Are

Constructs are the Clade’s most visible technological achievement, automatons built from metal, stone, and glass, animated by **loaded Amalga** that yields functional behavior without consciousness.

They are Gloamvere City’s labor force, border patrol, infrastructure maintenance crew, and most unsettling reminder that memory does not need a person to move through the world.

Every construct is, at base, **someone’s procedure** trapped in a chassis: a dead person’s reflexes (or a living donor’s sold skill) made to work without knowing why.

---

### How Constructs Work

#### The Chassis

The physical body: brass, steel, stone, and glass. The chassis is mechanical, gears, joints, counterweights, hydraulics. Thermal energy is commonly supplied by **sealed low-grade burners** (Caput Mortuum for bulk units, higher-grade fuel for sensitive assemblies), and some Clade facilities use centralized power lines (district infrastructure) rather than individual burners.

**Chassis determines what it *can* do.** 
**Amalga determines what it *does*.**

Chassis design reflects function:
- **Vigil-class** patrol constructs: endurance + threat response, reinforced joints, mounts.
- **Factor-class** maintenance constructs: precision articulation, tool slots, flexible spines.
- **Lumen-class** streetlights: barely constructs, fixtures with stabilized light chambers and minimal moving parts.

#### The Core (Loaded Amalga Matrix)

A sealed chamber in center-mass contains a **Loaded Amalga Matrix**, multiple Amalga phials wired into a control framework that translates procedural memory into mechanical action.

Examples:
- Patrol constructs: marching, restraint, weapon handling, pursuit, route execution.
- Maintenance constructs: diagnostics, patching, clearing, calibration loops.
- Refinery constructs: sorting, material handling, temperature monitoring sequences.

**Source quality matters.** 
A veteran’s Amalga yields fluid precision. A novice’s Amalga yields competence without finesse. Procurement premiums for “clean, high-skill Amalga” create a secondary market that targets skilled workers, one of the Conclave’s enduring objections.

#### The Absence (By Design)

Constructs are intentionally built without:
- **Tinctura** (emotion)
- **Quintessa** (identity)
- broad **Precipia** (general knowledge)

They do not *understand*; they *execute*. A Vigil can disarm someone but cannot explain why. A maintenance unit can repair a pipe but does not know what water *is*.

**Clade doctrine:** the absence of emotion/identity makes constructs tools, not slaves. 
**Conclave doctrine:** the Amalga is living spiritus, absence does not make it less alive, only less able to express suffering.

#### The Uncanny Valley

Because constructs are animated by real procedural patterns, they move with disturbing familiarity. A construct loaded with a known swordmaster’s Amalga fights with that master’s style, footwork, feints, hesitations. Someone who knew the origin might recognize them in the movement.

The construct has no awareness of this. It is performing reflexes in a body that is not theirs.

### Construct Classifications (Public)

| Class | Purpose | Memory Load | Behavior Level | Prevalence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lumen** | Lighting / fixed service nodes | Stabilized light load (often Volatilis; sometimes cold Precipia) | Passive output | Ubiquitous in Clade territory |
| **Factor** | Maintenance / logistics / clearing | Single-skill Amalga | Looping procedure | Common city-wide |
| **Vigil** | Patrol / border security | Multi-skill Amalga | Reactive criteria response | Common at borders |
| **Praetor** | Complex ops / investigation / relay | Dense Amalga + **limited Precipia** | Adaptive routing + prioritization | Rare |
| **Archon** | Experimental / classified | Unknown | Classified | Rumored |

#### Lumen

The simplest class: a sealed light chamber mounted on a brass fixture. Lumens have no meaningful decision-making. They are memory-fueled lamps with Clade engineering standards.

The Glass Spine is lit primarily by high-grade Lumens using stabilized, emotionally “cold” loads. Other districts rely more heavily on Conclave lanternwork or Caput Mortuum braziers.

#### Factor

The workhorse class. Single-purpose constructs loaded with one procedure set and run on loops:
- canal clearing
- patchwork and maintenance
- refinery handling
- tube calibration
- market logistics

Factors are the constructs most people see and stop noticing, until one malfunctions.

#### Vigil

Multi-skill load: combat Amalga (restraint, pursuit), recognition Amalga (pattern matching, gait heuristics), navigation Amalga (routes, obstacle avoidance).

Vigils enforce borders by executing criteria. They do not exercise judgment.
- They are **easy to fool** for someone who knows the criteria.
- They are **impossible to reason with** for someone who doesn’t.

#### Praetor

Dense multi-skill Amalga matrix supplemented with limited Precipia, enough contextual framing to:
- adjust routes to observed patterns
- prioritize targets by assessed risk
- coordinate multiple Vigils
- relay messages (often storing a temporary “voice print” as Volatilis)

Praetors are rare, expensive, and the closest thing to “intelligent” constructs the Clade publicly admits. Each Praetor is assigned a Logician handler responsible for monitoring drift, refreshing matrices, and reporting anomalies.

#### Archon (Classified / Rumored)

Archons are rumored to include **Tinctura** in their matrices, granting emotional response capability.

**Clade public position:** Tinctura-loaded constructs are a theoretical concept not pursued. 
**Street truth:** rumors persist because there are gaps in what Praetors can do and what certain classified units appear to do.

If Archons exist, they raise the same question Consonant masterworks do: at what point does a designed thing become a person? If a construct “fights like rage,” is it feeling anger or executing anger-shaped procedure?

### Long-Term Drift: “Patina” (Clade) / “Stirring” (Conclave)

The Clade’s Absence rule is doctrine, and, over long timescales, not perfectly stable.

#### What Happens to Old Constructs

A construct that runs the same route for years with the same matrix begins to exhibit behaviors that are not in the original protocol:
- micro-pauses at non-tactical points
- preferential handling patterns
- idiosyncratic rhythm shifts
- “habit-like” deviations that mirror the origin donor’s quirks

The Clade calls this **Patina**: accumulated behavioral noise from matrix wear, contamination, and minute degradation. Their official stance: maintenance issue. Refresh the Amalga. Replace the matrix.

The Conclave calls it **Stirring**: the spiritus responding to environment the way inhabited vessels respond to wielders. Their stance: the construct is not merely malfunctioning, it is *changing*.

Old Blood has no official position. Unofficially, the implication is distressing: if a construct can develop echo-habits, does that mean something of the origin persists, trapped?

### The Archon–Moree Intersection (Classified Speculation)

Clara Moree’s Living Memory research and the rumored Archon program approach the same question from different angles: **can you build something that develops identity?**

- **Archon path:** chassis + (rumored) emotional load → motivation → emergent self.
- **Moree path:** calx-based substrates → memory growth → emergent patterns → potential self.

If these intersect, if a chassis is loaded with a **Living Memory substrate** rather than bottled Amalga, the result would not execute someone else’s procedure. It would generate its own: learning, adapting, developing identity from its own experience.

If revealed, this would unite Conclave and Old Blood against the Clade:
- Conclave: the ultimate case of trapping spiritus.
- Old Blood: manufacturing people (and manufacturing souls, if you believe in them).

### Construct Economy

#### Who Makes Them

Constructs are manufactured by the Clade.
- **Chassis:** built by Factors (the human rank; the name overlap is deliberate Clade gallows humor).
- **Matrix assembly:** Engineers.
- **Programming & tuning:** Logicians.
- **Activation & authorization:** Adjutant or higher.

The Conclave refuses to manufacture constructs on philosophical grounds. Individual artisans have occasionally been contracted for Amalga quality assessment (their compasses read nuance better than Clade instruments). They charge accordingly.

#### Who Uses Them

- The Clade operates all security constructs directly.
- Factor-class maintenance units are commonly **leased (not sold)** to neutral infrastructure and, selectively, other factions.
- Old Blood leases minimal Factor units for tasks beneath human attention, under strict constraints and with ritual “cleaning” practices they pretend are not superstitious.

Construct leases are one of the Clade’s steadiest revenue streams and a subtle lever of influence: dependence is political.

#### The Amalga Supply Chain

Constructs consume Amalga. Matrices wear and require refresh.

Supply sources:
- voluntary donations (compensated)
- market purchases
- institutional extraction (Clade service obligations)

**Ethical boundary (official):** the Clade refuses coerced Amalga and refuses any Amalga tied to Calcination. 
**Practical boundary (unofficial):** black-market Amalga of uncertain provenance enters the chain through intermediaries, especially for low-requirement units where contamination “still works.”

Hollow Flame rhetoric points here: performative ethics at the front door, laundered inputs at the back.

### GM Levers

Use these as “dials,” not fixed truths:

- **Patina severity:** harmless quirks → escalating unpredictability → proto-personhood.
- **Archon reality:** myth → prototype → deployed but denied.
- **Moree overlap:** separate programs → accidental collision → deliberate integration.
- **Conclave stance:** philosophical objection only → active sabotage of construct lines → quiet rescue attempts for “stirring” units.
- **Old Blood stance:** avoidance → doctrinal condemnation → secret vault experiments on construct-captured procedures.
